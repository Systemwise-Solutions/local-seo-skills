# Orient: what the review job needs, and where to find it

Find each piece yourself first. Ask the user only for what you can't find, in one message, and say why you need it. If a brief exists, confirm it in one line and ask only about gaps.

| The job needs | Look here first | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| The profile and its reviews | A Maps link in the brief, a connected tool, a search for the business | Everything is measured from the reviews it has now. |
| The competitors beating it | The brief, `list_location_competitors`, a map pack check for the money searches | The target is set against them, not a generic number. |
| How reviews are asked for today | The user; review links or QR codes on the site or receipts; any review tool named in the repo or site | The failing step is usually here. |
| The moment the customer has the value | How a customer becomes revenue (lens): booking flow, checkout, delivery, job completion, handover | Decides when the ask goes out. |
| Where contact details live | Booking, job, till, ordering or CRM system named on the site or by the user | Decides whether the ask can be automatic, and by text or email. |
| Who is with the customer at that moment | The user | Owns the staff prompt and the card. |
| One-off or repeat customers | Site copy (memberships, courses, subscriptions, regular servicing), the user | Repeat customers are asked once in a while, not every visit. |
| Rules on what it can say or reply | A regulator, professional body or confidentiality promise named on the site; a compliance contact; brand rules | Decides what requests may mention and what replies may confirm. Find the rule before writing. |
| One location or many | Location pages, the profile list | One link and QR code per location, and reporting by branch. |
| Requests sent per month | The sending system's reports | Without it you can see reviews fell but not which step failed. |
| What's been tried | `local-seo-log.md`, the user | A past tool with a satisfaction filter, or a one-day blast, explains odd patterns. |

If the business can't capture a phone number or email at any point, say so plainly: the engine then relies on the QR code and staff, which is slower. Suggest the cheapest place to start capturing contact details (the booking form, the receipt, the invoice).

## The brief

Write or update `local-seo-brief.md` in plain labelled lines, under 40 lines. For this job, make sure it holds: how reviews are asked for today, where contact details live, when the customer has the value and who is there, any rules on what replies may say, and the competitors to measure against. Other skills read it, so keep it factual.
