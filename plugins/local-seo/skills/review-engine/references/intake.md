# Intake

Ask only what this skill needs and the brief doesn't already say. One message, options shown, so the user can answer fast ("1, 3b, 4a and 4c").

## Questions

1. **The business and its Google profile.** Name as on Maps, website, and the Maps link if handy.
2. **Who is this for?**
   a. My own business (one location)
   b. A brand with several locations
   c. An agency client (output goes to the client under the agency's brand)
3. **What kind of business?** Clinic or healthcare / trade or home services / hospitality / professional services / retail or automotive / other.
4. **How do you ask for reviews now?** Pick all that apply:
   a. We don't, really
   b. Staff mention it sometimes
   c. An email after the job or visit
   d. An SMS
   e. A card, sign or QR code
   f. A review tool or our booking system does it
5. **Where do customer phone numbers and emails live?** Booking system, job management app, till or card reader, spreadsheet, nowhere. Name the system if you know it.
6. **When does the job or appointment end, and who is there?** For example "engineer finishes on site, invoice sent that evening", or "patient pays at reception".
7. **Who you lose to.** 1 to 3 competitors that beat you in Google Maps for your main searches, if known.
8. **What you can share.** Review dates or a screenshot of the reviews tab, your replies, and any reviews you think are fake.

If they can't get customer phone numbers or emails at any point, say so plainly: the engine then relies on the QR code and staff, which is slower. Suggest the cheapest way to start capturing contact details (the booking form, the invoice).
