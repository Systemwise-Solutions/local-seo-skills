# Intake

Ask only what this skill needs and the brief doesn't already say. One message, options shown, so the user can answer fast ("1, 2b, 4a").

## Questions

1. **The profile.** Maps link, or the exact name as it shows on Google Maps. Plus the website.
2. **Who is this for?**
   a. My own business (one location)
   b. A brand with several locations
   c. An agency client (output goes to the client under the agency's brand)
3. **The name on your signage, van or registration.** Exactly as written. (This is what the profile name must match.)
4. **Do customers come to you?**
   a. Yes, to premises at the address
   b. No, we go to them (service-area business)
   c. Both
5. **The searches that make money.** Up to 5, as customers type them. Which one pays most?
6. **Where customers come from.** Town, postcodes or "about X miles".
7. **Who you lose to.** 1 to 3 competitors, if known. If not, I'll find them.
8. **Your access.** Owner / manager / none yet. (Manager is enough to make the changes. The owner should stay the owner.)
9. **What you can paste or screenshot.** The profile's edit screen (categories, services, description, hours, attributes), the performance tab, and which service pages exist on the site.
10. **Anything recent.** A move, rebrand, new phone number, suspension, or a past agency that "optimised" the profile.

If the profile is suspended, stop: optimisation waits until it is reinstated. Fix the cause first (usually the name, the address or a duplicate), then appeal with evidence. Changing other fields on a suspended profile doesn't help and muddies the appeal.

If they have 6 or more locations, work on a sample of 3 (strongest, weakest, typical), then group the changes by cause across the rest.
