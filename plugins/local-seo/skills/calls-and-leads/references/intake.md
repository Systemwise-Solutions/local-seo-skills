# Intake

Ask everything in one message, with options so the user can answer fast ("2a, 6c"). Skip anything the brief or their request already answers. If a `local-seo-brief.md` exists, only ask the lead questions (7 to 12).

## Questions

1. **Business name and website.** Plus the Business Profile name as it shows on Google Maps.
2. **Who is this for?**
   a. My own business
   b. A brand with several locations
   c. An agency client (the report goes to the client under the agency's brand)
3. **How many locations?** 1 / 2 to 5 / 6 or more. For more than one, which locations are worst.
4. **What kind of business?** Clinic or healthcare / trade or home services / hospitality / professional services / retail or automotive / something else.
5. **The searches that make money.** Up to 5, as customers type them.
6. **Where customers come from.** A town, postcodes, or how far.
7. **What's down?** Pick all that apply: a. calls b. form enquiries c. online bookings d. walk-ins or direction requests e. all of it f. the numbers look fine but the work isn't coming in
8. **When did it start, and what changed around then?** New website, new phone system or number, staff changes, new opening hours, price changes, profile edits, Google ads paused, moved premises.
9. **How are calls answered?** a. Whoever is free b. One person or reception c. Mobile, while on jobs d. Answering service or virtual receptionist e. Calls go to voicemail out of hours
10. **What counts as a booking?** A job in the diary, an appointment in the booking system, a paid deposit, a signed quote.
11. **What you can share for two periods** (the last month or quarter, and the same period before the drop or a year earlier):
    - Business Profile performance: views, calls, website clicks, directions, bookings (from the profile's Performance screen)
    - Phone system or call tracking: calls answered, calls missed, by hour if possible
    - Form enquiries received, and online bookings
    - Booked jobs or appointments
12. **Tracking in place.** a. Call tracking number on the profile b. UTM on the profile website link c. GA4 with key events for calls, forms or bookings d. None of these e. Not sure

## How the answers change the work

| Answer | What changes |
|---|---|
| Agency client | White-label report. Fixes owned by "agency team" and "client". Note for the account team: what the client must change themselves (phone answering, hours, staff). |
| Several locations | Run the funnel per location for the worst 3 and a good one. Look for one cause shared across locations (same booking widget, same call centre). |
| New website around the drop (8) | Page converts first: test the form, the call links and the booking path on a phone. If organic visibility also fell, hand to SEO for AI Agents (`rescue` or `migrate` job) for the site side. |
| New phone system or number (8) | Reached and Counted first: is the profile number right, does it ring through, what happens out of hours. Check the number on the main directories too. |
| Answered on a mobile while on jobs (9c) | Expect missed calls. Ask for the missed-call log; plan call-back or an answering service. |
| Numbers fine, work not coming in (7f) | Booked stage and lead quality first: are the enquiries for the jobs they want, in the area they serve? |
| No tracking (12d, 12e) | Tracking starts on day one. The first 30 days set the baseline as much as they measure fixes. |
| Regulated sector | Clinics: call recordings and enquiry data are patient data; don't ask for them in chat. Ask for counts only. |
