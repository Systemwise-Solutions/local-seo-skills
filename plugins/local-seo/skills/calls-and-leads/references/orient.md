# Orient: what the leads diagnosis needs, and where to find it

Find each piece yourself first. Ask the user only for what you can't find, in one message, and say why you need it. Never treat something you couldn't get as zero.

| The diagnosis needs | Look here first | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| How a customer becomes revenue, and which route brings most | The site: phone numbers, contact and quote forms, booking or reservation widgets, online ordering, "visit us" pages; the profile's booking, order or menu links; the brief | Decides the funnel fields, what "answered" and "converted" mean, and which stage to test first. |
| What's down, in the owner's words | The conversation | Calls, forms, bookings, orders, walk-ins, or "the numbers look fine but the work isn't coming in". Each points at a different stage. |
| When it started, and what changed around then | `local-seo-log.md`, the site's change history or repo commits, the profile's edit history; then the user | A new website, phone system, booking or ordering tool, staff, hours, prices, profile edits, paused ads or a move. The change near the drop is the first suspect. |
| How enquiries are handled | The user, the site's stated hours and response promises | Who answers calls, who replies to forms, how bookings are confirmed, what happens out of hours. Decides what to check in Reached. |
| What counts as revenue | The user, the booking or order system | A job in the diary, a kept appointment, a seated table, a paid order, a sale, a signed quote. The outcome field in `funnel.py`. |
| Counts for two comparable periods | A connected tool (profile performance, analytics), then the user's exports: profile performance, phone log, form inbox, booking, reservation or order system, till or door counts | The funnel. Same months a year apart if the business is seasonal. |
| What tracking exists | The site's code or tag setup if you can see it, the profile's phone and link, analytics; then the user | Call tracking on the profile, UTM on the profile link, key events for the main actions, source recorded on bookings or orders. Gaps here become day-one fixes. |
| Customers come to the business, or it goes to them | Address shown or hidden on the profile, site copy | Walk-in businesses lean on directions and visits, which are hard to count; travelling businesses lean on calls and quotes. |
| Rules on what it can say or collect | Any regulator named on the site, the brief | Shapes copy and what data may be shared. Ask for counts only where enquiry data is sensitive. |
| One location or many | Location pages, profiles | Many: run the funnel for the worst few and a good one, and look for one shared cause (the same booking tool, the same call centre). |
| Who the report is for | The conversation, the brief | Owner, in-house team or agency client changes the wording and the owners named, not the method. |

## How the answers change the work

| What you found | What changes |
|---|---|
| A new website around the drop | Page converts first: test the form, call links and booking or order path on a phone. If organic visibility also fell, the site side belongs to SEO for AI Agents (`rescue` or `migrate`). |
| A new phone system, number or booking tool | Reached and Counted first: is the profile pointing at the right number or link, does it ring through or confirm, what happens out of hours. |
| Enquiries handled by someone also doing the work | Expect missed calls and slow replies. Ask for the missed-call or response log; plan a call-back routine, a missed-call text or cover for peak times. |
| Numbers fine, revenue not coming in | Converted and lead quality first: are the enquiries for what the business sells, in the area it serves, at a price it can do? |
| Revenue mostly from walk-ins | Directions and the profile's hours, photos and reviews carry most of the weight. Count visits however the business can (till, door counter, asking "how did you find us") and read the rate as a trend. |
| No tracking | Tracking starts on day one. The first 30 days set the baseline as much as they measure fixes. |

## Writing the brief

Write what you learned to `local-seo-brief.md` in plain lines, under 40 lines: the business, the shape from the lens, money searches, area, competitors, audience, data available, constraints. Add a short "Leads" note: how a customer becomes revenue, what is counted where, when the drop started and what changed, who handles enquiries, what tracking exists. Other skills read it, so use plain labels rather than a fixed form. If a brief exists, update it rather than replacing it.
