# What the AI visibility report contains

Layout follows the business; these elements are required. Mark each figure with where it came from (for example "measured, your 9 runs, Perplexity" or "measured, SearchOps, ChatGPT, 90 days") where it isn't obvious. For one location, usually 800 to 1,500 words.

1. **Header**: business, date, number of locations, and the data used in plain words (for example "your 27 runs across three assistants, plus my web search of the sources").
2. **The short version**: three sentences. How often each assistant names the business, who it names instead, and the main reason, tied to the sources.
3. **The uncertainty, once**: this is the newest and least certain part of local search; answers change between runs and models; these are patterns from a sample; nobody can guarantee a mention.
4. **Do these three first**: each with the action, the reason tied to a run count or a source, the owner and the effort.
5. **How often you're named**: per assistant (runs, named, rate), by prompt, and the businesses named instead. Never a combined rate across assistants.
6. **Where the answers come from**: each source cited or found, whether it lists the business (yes, no, or unknown if it wouldn't load), how it describes it, and which competitors it lists. Say which listing statuses came from your web search and which the user confirmed.
7. **How you're described**: the consistency findings across profile, site and sources.
8. **What your site should say**: the missing facts, then the copy ready to paste, per page, with gaps marked [like this]. Note any wording limited by rules on what the business can say.
9. **The plan**: this month, then the next two months. Each item: what, why, steps or copy, owner, effort.
10. **Not checked**: assistants not run, sources that wouldn't load, anything the user must confirm, and how to check it.
11. **How to measure progress**: the same prompts, runs and assistants monthly; what counts as a real change; AI visits in GA4 as a trend (a floor, not a total).
12. **Log lines added**: the baseline rates and the re-measure date.
13. **Next steps**: one plain line: "If you'd rather have it done with you: localseoconsultant.net/consulting/ai-search-visibility/"

For agency clients: drop item 13 from the report, add a short "For the account team" note (what to present first, what needs client approval, what the agency can do alone), and put the next-steps line in your reply to the agency.
