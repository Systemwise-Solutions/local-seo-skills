# Intake for AI visibility

Ask only what's missing, in one message, with options. If the user has already answered in their request, don't ask again.

## Questions (only if there is no brief, or it lacks these)

1. **Business name and website.** Plus the name as it shows on Google Maps, and any other names it has traded under.
2. **Who is this for?** a. My own business / b. A brand with several locations / c. An agency client (white-label)
3. **What kind of business?** Clinic or healthcare / trade or home services / hospitality or leisure / professional services / retail or automotive / something else
4. **The searches that make money.** Up to 5, as customers type them.
5. **Where customers come from.** Town, neighbourhoods, or "about X miles".
6. **Who you lose to.** 1 to 3 competitors, if known.
7. **Which assistants do your customers use?** ChatGPT / Gemini / Google AI Overviews or AI Mode / Perplexity / Copilot / not sure (default: ChatGPT, Gemini and Google).
8. **What prompted this?** a. A customer said an assistant recommended someone else / b. I asked one and we weren't named / c. Visits from AI have dropped / d. Just checking

## Brief template

Write the brief in this exact shape (shared by every skill in the pack) and keep it under 40 lines. Add the assistants and prompt set under Notes so the next run reuses them.

```markdown
# Local SEO brief: <business name>

Updated: <date>
Audit for: <owner | brand | agency client (agency: <name>)>
Website: <url>
Profile: <Maps link or exact name>
Sector: <sector>
Locations: <count>, <list or sample>

## Money searches
1. <search>
...

## Market
Customers come from: <area>
Competitors to beat: <names, or "to find">

## Problems reported
- <problem in the user's words>

## Data available
- Tier: <free | MCP: name | DataForSEO key>
- Shared by user: <list, or none>

## Notes
- <anything that changes the audit: suspension, rebrand, recent move, regulated sector>
- AI assistants checked: <list>
- AI prompt set: <file name or the prompts>
```
