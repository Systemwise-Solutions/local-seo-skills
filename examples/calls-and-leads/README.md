# Example: a fictional roofing company whose phone went quiet

Example Roofing, Sutton, does not exist. The numbers are made up to show the skill working.

The scenario: an established roofer whose calls and quote requests fell after a new website went live in May 2026 and the office manager left in June. Rankings held.

```
S=../../plugins/local-seo/skills/calls-and-leads/scripts
python $S/funnel.py funnel.json > funnel-output.md
```

- `local-seo-brief.md`: the brief the intake produces.
- `funnel.json`: Business Profile performance, phone log, quote inbox and diary counts for Aug to Oct 2025 and 2026, the script's input.
- `observations.md`: what the owner reported and what the skill checked on the profile and homepage.
- `funnel-output.md`: the script's output.
- `output.md`: the full output: where the leak is, fixes in order, and the 30-day measurement plan.
