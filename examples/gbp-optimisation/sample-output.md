## The short version
Your profile tells Google you're a general plumber open 24 hours, with "boiler repair" and "Walthamstow" in the name, and lists "Plumbing" and "Heating" as services. The two businesses beating you for boiler installation call themselves heating contractors and list boiler installation, repair and servicing by name. The name is the urgent bit: keywords in the name are one of the most common reasons trade profiles get suspended, so it comes out first, then the category, then services and copy, one sensitive edit a week over four weeks.

## Make these three first
1. **Take the keywords out of the business name.** The profile says "Example Heating Co | Boiler Repair Walthamstow 24/7"; your vans, invoices and Companies House say "Example Heating Co". The extra words risk a suspension, and a suspended profile loses every call until it's reinstated. Effort: low.
2. **Change the primary category from Plumber to Heating contractor.** 2 of the 3 businesses beating you use it (reported, from your screenshots), and installs are your highest-value job. It's the strongest relevance signal you control. Effort: low.
3. **Name your three money searches as services.** Boiler installation, boiler repair and boiler service aren't on the profile at all; competitors list boiler repair (3 of 3) and installation and servicing (2 of 3). Effort: low.

Before step 1, fix the hours. "Open 24 hours" is bringing 2am calls you can't take, and two of your last ten reviews complain about it. It's a low-risk edit, so it goes in on day one.
