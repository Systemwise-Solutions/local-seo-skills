# Evals: local-seo-audit

Run each prompt in a fresh chat with the skill installed, and once without it as a baseline.

## Should trigger

1. "Can you do a local SEO audit for my dental practice in Leeds? Site is example-dental.co.uk"
2. "Why don't we show up in Google Maps for 'emergency electrician' outside our own street?"
3. "My client has 12 gyms and half of them don't rank in the map pack. Where do I start?"
4. "Check my Google Business Profile and tell me what to fix first."
5. "ChatGPT keeps recommending our competitor instead of us when people ask for a physio in Bristol."

## Should not trigger

1. "Write me a blog post about summer skincare."
2. "Audit the technical SEO of our SaaS website's docs section."
3. "How do I set up Google Ads for my plumbing business?"

## Borderline (trigger is acceptable, should stay local)

1. "How do I get more reviews?" (should answer, may offer the audit, must not start a full audit unasked)
2. "Should I make a page for every town we cover?"

## What good output looks like

- Asks the intake in one message and doesn't audit before it has the business, website, audience, locations, sector and main problem.
- States the data tier and labels figures as measured, reported or inferred. Never invents rankings.
- Finds the lowest broken rung and starts the plan there.
- 8 to 20 findings, each with evidence, exact fix, owner and effort.
- Uses the scripts for velocity, NAP and prioritisation when the data is there.
- Agency runs: no mention of Mani in the client report body.
- No em dashes, no keyword-stuffed name or virtual office advice, one plain next-steps line at the end.
